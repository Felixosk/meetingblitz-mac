Everything it does

Small app, many details. All of it works without an account.

Banner before every event

On all monitors, lead time of your choice. Overlapping events each get their own banner.

27 motifs, 3 styles

Submarine, whale, jet, UFO and more. Double-click one to read its real story, with a source.

Widget in the menu bar

Timeline, week and month view, any day one click away.

One-click join

53 services: Meet, Zoom, Teams, Webex, Whereby, Jitsi, Discord, Slack huddles, GoTo and more.

New meeting in one line

Day, time and length are read from your text. Drag the meeting in the day strip.

Conflict warning

Shows which event is in the way before you book the slot.

Email to guests

Optional, through your own Gmail. Up to 5 guests, one mail to all, text editable per meeting.

Instant meeting

A Google Meet room right now, with 5 seconds to undo.

Time zones

Add cities in the New Meeting window, rename them, see each city's time.

Copy an event

Paste into WhatsApp and it becomes a calendar file. Paste into a text field and it becomes text.

Apple Reminders

Reminders due today sit next to your events.

Per calendar control

Choose what shows, what fires a banner and whether birthdays appear.

Declined stays quiet

Declined invitations never warn you. They stay visible, struck through.

Quiet mode

For 1 hour, 5 hours, a day or a week, and on its own while you share your screen.

Two shortcuts

Open the widget, join the current or next call. Both can be changed.

Meeting stats

How many meetings per week, month and year.

Automation

meetingblitz://show, join-next, instant, create?text=…, settings

Claude access

Optional MCP server: Claude can list, create and move your events.

Light and dark

Follows your Mac or set by hand. Glass Normal or Clearer.

English and German

Switch any time in Settings.

Not a submarine person? Fine.

Swap the submarine for a whale, a jet or a UFO: 27 motifs for the banner, or let them take turns. Everything that belongs in the sea leaps out of the sea, everything that belongs in the sky bursts out of a cloud.

Every motif is a real thing: a real boat, a real animal, a real aircraft. Double-click one in the app and its true story opens, with a source.

How it compares

If you want the most mature option with the most services, install MeetingBar. It is excellent, and this project started as a fan of it.
