Free for personal use · macOS 14 or newer · Apple Silicon and Intel

Five things it does for you

It lives in the menu bar. No window, no Dock icon, no account.

1 · The banner

A warning you can't scroll past

Notifications slide into a corner and wait in a list. This one flies across every monitor, with the title, the time and a Join button.

Two meetings at once? You get two banners, one above the other. Do Not Disturb does not hide it, because the app draws it itself.

2 · The widget

Your day in one click

Click the icon in the menu bar. You see what is running now, what comes next, a timeline from 0 to 24 and today's birthdays with a call button.

Join, copy the link or copy the whole event. The arrows step through weeks and months.

3 · New meeting

Type it in one line

Write call with chris sat 3:30pm 45min. Day, time and length are picked out while you type and marked in blue.

The day strip shows what else is on. Drag the new meeting to move it. If the slot is taken, it says so before you book.

4 · After creating

The rest happens by itself

Above the button you tick what comes next: email the invite to up to 5 guests through your Gmail, copy the invite text, save a calendar file.

The button says exactly what it will do. You can change the email for this one meeting before it goes out.

5 · Instant meeting

A call right now, with a way back

The lightning button makes a Google Meet room, puts it in your calendar and opens the call.

Pressed it by mistake? You have 5 seconds to undo, and the event is gone again.

Light or dark, your call

It follows your Mac, or you pick Light or Dark yourself. The glass can be set clearer for busy wallpapers.

Stays on your Mac

No server, no account, no tracking. It reads the calendars your Mac already has: iCloud, Google, Exchange.

Native and small

Swift and SwiftUI, no Electron. You can read every line on GitHub and build it yourself in two minutes.

Joins 53 services

Meet, Zoom, Teams, Webex, Whereby, Jitsi, Discord, Slack huddles and more, with one click.

Put it in your menu bar

Download, unzip, move to Applications. One command on first launch.
